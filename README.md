## 我相信 / What I Believe

> **增长不是把更多人带进来，而是让正确的用户更快感受到价值，并愿意继续回来。**  
> Growth isn't about bringing more people in — it's about helping the right users feel value faster, and want to keep coming back.

> **出海也不只是翻译，而是在新的市场里重新理解用户。**  
> Going global isn't just translation — it's understanding users anew in a new market.

**很多项目都从一个很小的问题开始：一张难填的表、一场来不及整理的活动、一个反复讲不清楚的商业故事。**  
Many projects start from a tiny problem: a form that's hard to fill out, an event there was no time to recap, a business story that never quite comes across.

**我想做的，是让 AI 离真实用户和真实工作再近一点。**  
What I want is to bring AI a little closer to real users and real work.
